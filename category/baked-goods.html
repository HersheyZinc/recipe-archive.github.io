Brownies

Published: Mon 09 March 2026
By Zhe Xun

In baked goods.

image: /images/brownies.jpg

ingredients: - name: butter amount: 100 unit: g - name: sugar amount: 200 unit: g - name: cocoa powder amount: 500 unit: g - name: eggs amount: 2 unit: pcs

  1. Melt 100 g butter.
  2. Mix with 200 g sugar.
  3. Add cocoa powder and eggs.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 20 minutes.
