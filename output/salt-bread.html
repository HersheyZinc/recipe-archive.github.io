Salt Bread

Salt Bread

baking

Yield

rolls(s)

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. In the mixing bowl, add all the ingredients and mix with a dough hook for 10 mins until the dough comes together (dough will be sticky).
  2. Remove the dough from mixer and slap and fold until it comes together (~3-5mins), cover and rest for 15min .
  3. 1st stretch and fold on all 4 sides, cover and rest for 15min
  4. 2nd stretch and fold on all 4 sides
  5. Rest until the dough doubles in size (~1.5h)
  6. After 1.5h of bulk proofing, using a scale, cut and measure 80g balls of dough
  7. Roll each ball and tuck in the bottom (stretch the skin)
  8. Place on baking paper on a tray, cover and rest until it doubles in size (~30mins)
  9. Cut the butter up into 10-12g blocks and freeze before use
  10. Take the ball of dough and roll it into a log. Roll down once with the rolling pin (thin) and roll up (increasing width toward the top), place the butter block at the top, and roll the butter in the centre of the roll.
  11. Preheat the oven to 200c, no fan
  12. Rest until the rolls double in size again (~30min)
  13. Spray the rolls with water and top with flaky salt
  14. Bake the bread rolls for 20mins at 200c.
  15. Melt salted butter ~3mins before the bread is ready to come out of the oven
  16. Once the bread is out, immediately brush the rolls with melted butter.
  17. Allow to cool, serve and enjoy.

Tips: ⁠ ⁠When removing dough from mixer after the first 10min mixer-knead, wet your hands to prevent sticking Remember to flour your working surface when kneading, cutting and rolling to prevent sticking ⁠⁠If the dough is too sticky, add bread flour bit by bit, if it is too dry, more water on your hands should do the trick
