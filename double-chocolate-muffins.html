Double Chocolate Muffins

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C.
  2. Beat the eggs, then combine with the oil, milk, water and vanilla.
  3. Add sour cream, cake mix, pudding mix, and cinnamon. Stir just until combined; fold in the batter chocolate chips.
  4. Portion into cases, filling up to three quarters. Scatter the topping chips over each muffin.
  5. Bake for 15–20 minutes, checking the centres for doneness.

Batch yield

Measured yield: 30 ice cream scoops of batter, making 30 small muffins (one scoop each) or 15 large muffins (two scoops each). Large muffins would need more baking time.
