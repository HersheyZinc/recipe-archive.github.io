1. In a big bowl, combine flour + yeast. 
2. Add water, mix with a spoon until flour is worked in 
3. Add salt and olive oil, and gently mix in (it will be goopy). Let it rest for 15 mins
4. Do 4 sets of coil folds every 25 minutes 
5. With wet hands, lift one side of the dough, let it stretch and fold it under itself. Rotate and repeat on all sides. Cover with damp towel between sets
6. Cover the bowl tightly and refrigerate for 12-48hrs. It should rise a bit and be full of bubbles. 24 hours is a good amount of time 
7. Generously oil your pan (don’t skimp, at least 2-3tbsp) 
8. Gently pour the cold dough into the pan. Don’t deflate it. Add a drizzle of olive oil on top.
9. Let it proof at room temp for 4-5hrs until it’s puffy and bubbly 
10. Preheat oven to 232℃ 
11. With oiled fingers, dimple the dough. Top with flaky salt, rosemary, garlic. 
12. Drizzle more olive oil on top to help crisp the surface
13. Bake for 20-25mins until deeply golden and crisp 
14. Cool in pan for at least 10min before removing
