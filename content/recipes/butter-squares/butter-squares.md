1. Place baking pan in the freezer for at least 2 hours. *Do not grease the pan*.
2. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
3. In a mixer, mix the butter, egg, sugar, salt and vanilla together until creamy.
4. Add the flour and mix by hand until the mixture holds together in large clumps.
5. Press the dough onto the chilled and ungreased baking pan until it is even. Dust the top evenly with raw/coarse sugar.
6. Bake for 12-15 minutes until edges turn golden brown.
7. Remove from oven and let cool for 5 minutes before cutting the cooked dough into squares.
