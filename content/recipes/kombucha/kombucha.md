1. Steep the black tea in boiling water for 5 minutes. Let it cool to room temperature.
2. In a large fermentation vessel, mix the sugar and remaining water until dissolved.
3. Pour black tea and starter tea into fermentation vessel. Stir thoroughly.
4. Finally, add the scoby into the vessel and cover loosely.
5. Store vessel at room temperature for 1 week. 

Note: Fermentation time decreases with higher ambient temperature. The average room temperature used here is 30 C.
