Pineapple Buns

Hong Kong bolo bao with soft sponge-method dough and a crisp, buttery topping

baking

Yield

bun(s)

Ingredients

  • Sponge

  • Final dough

  • Pineapple topping

    Instructions

    1. Mix all the sponge ingredients together: bread flour, sugar, milk powder, beaten egg, milk and instant yeast. Cover and leave to ferment until expanded and airy, with a web-like structure inside, around 60-90 minutes.
    2. Add the final-dough bread flour, sea salt and softened vegetable shortening to the sponge. Knead until smooth and elastic, aiming for a dough temperature of 25–26°C after kneading.
    3. Cover and leave to rise until roughly doubled. The original recipe allows 45–60 minutes.
    4. Gently press out the gas and divide into equal portions, around 75g. Round each portion with your palm, cover with a damp cloth and rest for 10 minutes.
    5. Gently round the portions again as needed and place on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Cover loosely with plastic wrap and proof somewhere gently warm, around 26–28°C, until noticeably puffy and a gentle fingertip indentation springs back slowly, around 45 minutes.
    6. While the buns proof, prepare the topping. Mix the topping cake flour, sugar, baking soda and optional baker's ammonia with the topping milk. Add the softened butter and topping egg yolk, then mix and gently knead until smooth. Divide into one portion per bun.
    7. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
    8. Lay plastic wrap on the work surface. Place a portion of topping dough on it, flatten slightly, then use a dough scraper to press it into a thin sheet. Repeat with the remaining portions.
    9. Lightly spray the proofed buns with water, then carefully lay a sheet of topping over each bun. Brush lightly with the extra egg yolk.
    10. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the topping is golden. Transfer to a wire rack to cool slightly before serving.

    Notes

    Original recipe called for sugar tolerant yeast and lard, so proofing might take longer than instructed.
