Sugar Wash

Unflavoured alcohol fermented from sugar. Can be flavoured or infused with other ingredients after fermentation.

Instructions

  1. Rehydrate yeast in lukewarm water.
  2. Mix water and sugar together in fermentation vessel until completely dissolved. Add 1/3 of the Fermax as the first addition.
  3. Pitch yeast into must.
  4. At 24 hours, add 1/3 of the Fermax as second addition.
  5. at 48 hours, add 1/3 of the Fermax as third addition.
